## The 8 Checks

### 1. Privacy Policy (Privacy Notice)
> *Saaf bhaasha mein batao: kaunsa data, kyon, kitne din, kisse share*

Before you collect anyone's personal data, you must tell them what you are collecting and why, in a notice they can understand on its own.

- [x] A Privacy Policy page is linked in the footer of every page and next to every form.
- [x] It lists each type of data you collect (name, phone, email, address, IP, cookies, payment info).
- [x] It says the specific purpose for each (order delivery, support, marketing, analytics).
- [x] It says how long you keep the data and who you share it with (payment gateway, courier, CRM, ad platforms).
- [x] It explains how users can withdraw consent, use their rights and complain to the Data Protection Board.
- [x] It describes the security practices you follow, and shows a "last updated" date.

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.5` · `DPDP Rules r.3` · `IT Rules 2011 r.4`

---

### 2. Consent, Done Properly
> *Pehle se tick? Nahi chalega. Waapas lena bhi utna hi aasaan.*

Consent must be free, specific, informed and given by a clear action. Silence or a pre-ticked box is not consent.

- [x] No pre-ticked boxes on any form: not for marketing, not for WhatsApp updates, not for purchases.
- [x] Marketing consent is separate from the consent needed to deliver the service.
- [x] Users can withdraw consent as easily as they gave it (an unsubscribe link, a settings toggle).
- [x] You keep a record of who consented, to what, and when.

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.6` · `DPDP Rules r.3` · `E-Commerce Rules r.4(9)`

---

### 3. Grievance Officer & Contact
> *Shikaayat sunne wale ka naam aur contact, website par saaf*

People must know exactly whom to contact about their data or an order, and get an answer in time.

- [x] The name, designation and contact (email + phone) of a Grievance Officer or the person who answers data questions is on the website.
- [x] There is a simple way to raise a complaint (form or email), ideally with a ticket number.
- [x] Data complaints are answered within 90 days at the latest (DPDP); online-shopping complaints are acknowledged in 48 hours and resolved in 1 month.

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.8(9), s.8(10)` · `DPDP Rules r.9, r.14` · `IT Rules 2011 r.5(9)` · `E-Commerce Rules r.4`

---

### 4. Cookies & Tracking
> *Visitor ko batao ki aap track kar rahe ho, aur uski marzi lo*

Analytics tags and ad pixels collect personal data such as IP address, device and behaviour. That needs notice and consent like any other data.

- [x] A cookie banner appears on the first visit, with Accept, Reject and Choose options that are equally easy to click.
- [x] Non-essential trackers (ads, analytics) load only after the visitor agrees.
- [x] Your Privacy Policy names the tools you use and why (analytics, ads, chat widgets).
- [x] Visitors can change their choice later (a "Cookie settings" link in the footer).

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.5, s.6` · `Best practice`

---

### 5. Terms & Conditions
> *Service ke niyam, likhit mein. Kal jhagda ho, toh aap surakshit.*

Written terms protect you in a dispute and set clear expectations for customers.

- [x] A Terms & Conditions page covers your services, pricing and payment, cancellations, and limits of liability.
- [x] It states the governing law and jurisdiction (your city's courts) and how disputes are handled.
- [x] If users can post content (reviews, comments, listings), it includes user rules and how you handle complaints.
- [x] Users agree to it at sign-up or checkout (an unticked box or a clear "By continuing…" line).

**Legal basis:** `Contract Act, 1872` · `IT Intermediary Rules 2021 r.3 (if users post content)`

---

### 6. Selling Online? Policies + Business Details
> *Return, refund, shipping. Legal naam, pata, contact. Sab website par.*

Every e-commerce seller must show clear consumer information before the customer pays.

- [x] Return, Refund, Exchange, Shipping/Delivery and Warranty policies, including who pays for return shipping. *(Service cancellation & SLA terms)*
- [x] Your legal business name, registered address, customer care contact and Grievance Officer details.
- [x] The total price with a break-up of all charges before payment, plus accepted payment modes.
- [x] Product country of origin and other mandatory product details where they apply.
- [x] No dark patterns: no fake countdowns, hidden charges, items sneaked into the cart, or fake reviews.

**Legal basis:** `E-Commerce Rules 2020 r.4–6` · `CCPA Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023`

---

### 7. Children's Data (Under 18)
> *18 se kam umar? Maata-pita ki permission zaroori.*

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a child. Their data has the strictest rules.

- [x] Sign-up or enquiry forms ask for age (or date of birth / 18+ confirmation) where children could use your service.
- [x] For users under 18, you get verifiable parental consent before collecting their data.
- [x] No tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads aimed at children.
- [x] Nothing you do with a child's data is likely to harm their well-being.

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.9` · `DPDP Rules r.10` · **Penalty up to ₹200 cr**

---

### 8. Data Security & Breach Response
> *SSL lagao, data surakshit rakho. Leak hua? Dono ko batao.*

You must take reasonable security safeguards. This carries the highest penalty in the law, up to ₹250 crore.

- [x] The whole site runs on HTTPS (valid SSL certificate, no "Not secure" warning).
- [x] Form entries, orders and customer lists are stored with access control (only the people who need them), strong passwords and 2-step login.
- [x] Sensitive data is encrypted or masked, with regular backups, and access logs kept for at least 1 year.
- [x] Vendors that handle your data (hosting, CRM, agencies) have a contract that requires security.
- [x] A written breach plan: tell affected users without delay, and inform the Data Protection Board, with a detailed report within 72 hours.
- [x] Cyber incidents are reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them.

**Legal basis:** `DPDP Act s.8(5), s.8(6)` · `DPDP Rules r.6, r.7` · `CERT-In Directions 2022`

## Beyond the 8

- **User rights:** Let people ask for a summary of their data, correct it, delete it, and name a nominee (`DPDP Act s.11–14`).
- **Delete when done:** Erase personal data once its purpose is over (`DPDP Act s.8(7)`, `Rules r.8`).
- **Keep records:** Keep consent records and forensic logs for ≥1 year (`DPDP Rules r.6`).
- **Large platforms:** Erase inactive users' data after 3 years (`DPDP Rules r.8`).
